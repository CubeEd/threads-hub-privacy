Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Threads Automation Hub ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when using the Meta Threads API.

Threads Automation Hub is a content research and workflow tool operated by the authenticated user of the application.

2. Information We Access

When authorized through the Threads API, the App may access information permitted by the permissions granted to the App.

This may include:

  • Threads user ID associated with the authorized account
  • Threads username
  • Public Threads post IDs
  • Public post text
  • Public post permalinks
  • Post timestamps
  • Media type information
  • Public posts returned by keyword searches
  • Keywords entered by the App operator for content research

3. How We Use Information

Threads API data is used to provide content research and discovery functionality.

For example, the App may use the official Threads keyword search API to find public posts matching keywords selected by the authenticated App operator.

Search results may be filtered, deduplicated, organized, and displayed to the App operator for research and review.

Keyword search functionality itself does not publish, delete, or modify Threads content.

4. Data Storage

The App is primarily operated locally by the App owner. Data required for the application's workflow may be stored locally on the operator's computer.

The App is designed to minimize storage of Threads API data and retain only information needed to provide its functionality.

Sensitive authentication credentials, including Threads access tokens, are kept separately from normal application data and are not intentionally displayed in application logs or the user interface.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell Threads API data or personal information.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Information may be processed by Meta as necessary to provide the Threads API and Threads platform functionality.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the App, provide research functionality, prevent duplicate processing, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Local application data can be deleted by the App operator.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of the App by contacting us at the email address listed below.

Requests will be reviewed and applicable data controlled by the App will be deleted where appropriate.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards designed to protect application data and authentication credentials from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Access tokens and other credentials should not be publicly shared or committed to public source-code repositories.

9. Third-Party Services

The App uses the Meta Threads API. Use of Threads and Meta services is also subject to Meta's own terms, policies, and privacy practices.

Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's functionality, data handling practices, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
